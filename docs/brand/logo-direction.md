# LegalConnect NG Logo Direction

## Brand Position

LegalConnect NG should feel trustworthy, modern, Nigerian, and useful. The logo should work for a legal marketplace and an X-style professional network, so it needs to be simple enough for app navigation while still feeling credible in investor decks, lawyer forum announcements, and public launch materials.

## Recommended Logo Concepts

### 1. Scale + Connection Mark

A clean justice scale icon where the beam or base subtly forms a connection node. This keeps the legal signal immediate while suggesting marketplace/network activity.

Best for: strong legal recognition, public trust, easy favicon/app icon use.

### 2. LC Monogram With Subtle Scales

A custom `LC` monogram where the `L` anchors the mark and the `C` forms a circular connection path. A tiny balanced scale can be embedded only if it remains readable at small sizes.

Best for: premium brand feel, investor decks, long-term trademark distinctiveness.

### 3. Legal Chat/Brief Mark

A briefcase or speech bubble shape with a minimal scale inside it. This emphasizes legal help, messaging, and client-lawyer connection.

Best for: communicating the product workflow quickly.

## Recommended Direction

Use the **LC Monogram With Subtle Scales** as the primary brand mark, with a simplified scale-only variant for favicon and small mobile navigation. It will feel less generic than courthouse/gavel logos and can grow into a recognizable marketplace brand.

## Avoid

- Gavels as the primary symbol. They feel generic and court-heavy.
- Courthouse pillars. They suggest government/courts more than a modern marketplace.
- Complex seals, wreaths, or tiny document details. They will fail at 24px.
- Overly formal black-and-gold-only styling. The current product has a modern blue X-style identity.
- Stock-looking law firm logos.

## Color Guidance

- Primary: current brand blue `#1A8CD8`.
- Text: near-black `#0F1419`.
- Support: success green `#00BA7C`, premium gold `#FFD400`.
- The mark must work in one color, full color, dark-on-light, and light-on-dark.

## Designer Deliverables

- Primary horizontal logo: SVG and PNG.
- Standalone icon mark: SVG and PNG.
- Favicon/app icon: 16px, 32px, 180px, 512px.
- Social avatar version that reads inside a circle.
- Light and dark variants.
- One-color black and one-color white variants.
- Clear-space and minimum-size rules.

## Usage Requirements

- Must be readable at 24px in the sidebar/navbar.
- Must work as a mobile app icon or PWA icon.
- Must fit a circular social avatar.
- Must look credible on pitch decks and legal forum announcements.
- Must not rely on tiny text inside the icon.
